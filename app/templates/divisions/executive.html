Executive


The Executive Office consists of the Office of the Commissioner, Grants Unit, Operations Unit, and the EEO/MWBE coordinator.


The Commissioner’s Office sets direction for DORIS and ensures that agency operations advance our mission. The office analyzes processes and procedures and plans for future growth. We develop and implement policies, oversee compliance with City laws and directives, including the language and disability access requirements and report on the agency’s progress to the Mayor and City Council.


The Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO) Minority and Women Owned Business (MWBE) coordinator is a neutral, independent employee who investigates allegations of discrimination, harassment and retaliation filed by employees, applicants, contracted and temporary staff and members of the public based on protected categories covered by federal, state and local laws. The coordinator also is responsible for ensuring that DORIS follows City laws to increase procurements from MWBEs.


The Grants Unit coordinates applications to the State by City agencies for grants totaling City government’s applications for up to $1 million annually in State grants for projects to improve records management records at various agencies.


The Operations unit has wide-ranging responsibilities in facilities management, contracting, and coordinating large-scale interagency and intra-agency projects. This includes managing upgrades, repairs and renovations to DORIS facilities; surveying building conditions on a semi-annual basis to identify any emerging issues; preparing reports on the status of facilities; liaising with DCAS facilities and asset management and with Industry City management; and working on exhibit design and other public programming activities.


The Executive Office consists of:

  • Kenneth Cobb, Assistant Commissioner
  • Johnnie Davis, EEO/MWBE Coordinator
  • Michael Lorenzini, Operations Director
  • Eleanor Price, Grant Unit Supervisor
  • Gerald Rosero, Special Assistant
  • Pauline Toole, Commissioner
