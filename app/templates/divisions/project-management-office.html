Project Management Office


The Project Management Office’s mission is to organize DORIS projects to ensure the best use of the agency resources. Working with agency stakeholders, PMO will assess current and proposed projects, establish priorities and schedule available resources to meet agree-upon timelines. The PMO’s roles and responsibilities are the followings:

  • Manage high priority and cross divisional projects
  • Develop project management standards and best practices
  • Establish project governance
  • Stakeholder engagement
  • Resource management
  • Risk management
  • Communication and reporting
  • Training and support
  • Continuous improvement
