CityShare


New York City's Employee Portal. Access information about benefits for City Employees, Information Technology, and much more.

City Time


New York City's Employee Timeclock. Use this to manage your timesheet, enter time punches, and request leave.

Timeclock


New York City Department of Records timeclock for contractors and interns. Use this to clock in and out, generate timesheets, and print out invoices.

NYCAPS


NYCAPS - Employee Self-Service (ESS). NYC Employee Self-Service Portal. Find your pay stubs, deduction information, and manage your personal information in the city's online human resources portal.

NYC Employee Retirement System


New York City Employee Retirement System. Manage your retirement benefits and plan for your retirement.

NYC Payroll Administration


New York City Office of Payroll Administration website has information about direct deposit, paycheck calculator information on the commuter benefits program, city holidays, and pay dates.

NYC Office of Labor Relations


New York City Office of Labor Relations website allows you to find out information about NYC Health Benefit Plans description and cost, Flexible Spending Accounts, Deferred Compensation, and Management Benefit Fund (Managers only).

NYC Administrative Services


New York City Department of Citywide Administrative Services website has information about civil service exams and requirements, training at the Citywide Training Center, and information about working with and for the City of New York.
