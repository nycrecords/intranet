Divisions


Administration & Human Resources

The Administration & Human Resources division is responsible for all maintaining all employee personnel records, procurement, and payroll.

Executive

The Executive division ensures that all of the agency’s operations run smoothly and that we advance DORIS’s mission.

External Affairs

The External Affairs Unit coordinates the public-facing elements of the agency, including its websites, social media, public programs, education, community outreach and marketing.

Information Technology

The IT division is responsible for innovation, automation, achieving operational efficiencies, standardization, using industry best practices and maximizing the use of technology across the agency.

Municipal Archives

The Municipal Archives preserves and makes available the historical records of New York City government. The MA holds over 200,000 cubic feet and over 50TB of records dating from the early seventeenth century to the present.

Municipal Library

The Municipal Library is mandated by the New York City Charter as the official repository for New York City agency publications. They provide access through our print collection and electronic government documents portal.

Municipal Records Management

The Municipal Records Management division develops and sets citywide policies for records management and operate storage facilities that holds active records for NYC agencies.

Project Management Office

The Project Management Office’s mission is to organize DORIS projects to ensure the best use of the agency resources.
